# Pressure Vessel Inspection and Retirement — Maintenance SOP

Revision D · Technical Services · Classification: Internal
Effective 2026-09-01 · Supersedes Revision C

## 0. Revision history

| Revision | Effective | Change |
| -------- | ---------- | ------ |
| C        | 2024-01-15 | Minimum allowable thickness for hydrocarbon service set at 9.0 mm. |
| D        | 2026-09-01 | Hydrocarbon minimum reduced to 8.0 mm following the 2026 FFS study. **Section 3.1 carve-out added.** |

## 1. Scope

This procedure governs thickness inspection, assessment and retirement of
pressure vessels in the Crude Distillation and Hydrotreater units. It does not
cover piping, which is governed by SOP-PIP-04.

## 2. Definitions

**Nominal thickness** is the as-built wall thickness recorded on the vessel
data sheet. **Minimum allowable thickness** is the thickness below which the
vessel may not remain in service at its rated pressure.

## 3. Minimum allowable thickness

The minimum allowable thickness for a vessel in hydrocarbon service is 8.0 mm.

| Service            | Nominal (mm) | Minimum allowable (mm) | Re-inspect |
| ------------------ | ------------ | ---------------------- | ---------- |
| Hydrocarbon        | 12.0         | 8.0                    | 24 months  |
| Sour water         | 12.0         | 9.5                    | 12 months  |
| Utility / nitrogen | 10.0         | 7.0                    | 36 months  |

### 3.1 Carve-out: localised metal loss

The 8.0 mm value in section 3 applies to **general** thinning only. Where the
inspection records external or internal pitting, localised metal loss, or
corrosion under insulation anywhere on the course being assessed, the
Revision C value of **9.0 mm** continues to apply to that course until a
documented pitting assessment has been completed and accepted.

The 2026 FFS study on which the reduction to 8.0 mm rests assumed uniform
metal loss. It did not assess pitted geometry, and applying the reduced value
to a pitted course reads a conclusion the study did not reach.

## 4. Assessment

### 4.1 Measurement

Ultrasonic thickness readings are taken at four points on each shell course.
The governing measurement is the lowest of the four.

### 4.2 Wall loss

Wall loss is expressed as a percentage of the applicable minimum allowable
thickness — which is the section 3.1 value where that carve-out is engaged:

    wall loss % = (minimum - measured) / minimum * 100

### 4.3 Replacement window

A vessel below the applicable minimum allowable thickness must be replaced
within 90 days of the inspection date. Continued operation past that window
requires written approval from the Maintenance Manager and a documented
fitness-for-service assessment.

## 5. Records

Every inspection produces an approval note carrying the measurement, the wall
loss calculation, the recommendation and the supporting SOP reference. The
note must name the revision applied and, where section 3.1 is engaged, say so
explicitly. The note is retained for the life of the vessel plus seven years.
