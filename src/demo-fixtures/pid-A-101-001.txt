This is a text rendering of a synthetic P&ID, generated for the ARJUN
demonstration. It is not a drawing of any real plant. It is plain text rather
than a scanned image so that a reviewer can read exactly what the model was
given, and so the demonstration does not depend on OCR quality to show
retrieval and citation working.
